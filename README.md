# Coffee-Shop-Sales-Data-Analysis (Interactive Dashboard creation using MS Excel)
## Project Objective
You are recently become a franchise owner at Maven Roasters, a coffee shop chain with three locations in New York City. To Better Understand Purchase behavior and streamline operations you are collected transactional data from Jan-Jun 2023. Your Goal is to tranform the data into a dynamic dashboard the franchise owners can use to identify patterns, trends and opportunities for the business.

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/Saiyam2004/Data-Analysis-Dashboard-Project-3/blob/main/Coffee%20Shop%20Sales.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
- How do sales vary by vary of the week and hour of the day ?
- Are there any peak times for sales activity?
- What is the total sales revenue for jan to june month?
- How do sales vary across different store locations?
- What is the average price/order per person
- Which products are the best-selling in terms of quantity and revenue?
- How do sales vary by product category and type?

- Dashboard Interaction <a href="https://github.com/Saiyam2004/Data-Analysis-Dashboard-Project-3/blob/main/Coffee%20Shop%20Sales.png">View Dashboard</a>

## Process
- Verify data for any missing values and anomalies, and sort out the same.
- Made sure data is consistent and clean with respect to data type, data format and values used.
- Created pivot tables according to the questions asked.
- Merge all pivot tables into one dashboard and apply slicer to make dynamic.

## Project Insight
- By Day of Week: Sales are fairly consistent across weekdays (Sunday to Saturday)
- By Hour of Day: Sales peak strongly in the morning hours (7 AM – 10 AM)
- The total sales revenue for jan to june month is $698,812.33
- All three locations perform almost equally well, with Hell’s Kitchen slightly leading in total sales.
- Average Bill per Person: $4.69
- Average Orders per Person: 1.4
- Barista Espresso dominates in terms of sales revenue.
- Coffee is the core product driving sales, followed by tea. Bakery items also contribute significantly.
- Customers prefer regular or large sizes over small.
